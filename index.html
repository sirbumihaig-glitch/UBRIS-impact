01 / A DIFFERENT QUESTION

Full calendars.
Positive feedback.
Unclear impact?

Activity is often mistaken for impact. The problem is rarely effort. It is design.

Programmes are delivered. Participation metrics look healthy. Feedback is positive. Three or six months later, business indicators may not have moved, managers report limited behavioural change and old habits resurface.

Most learning functions were built to deliver experiences. Today, they are expected to deliver performance. That shift requires clearer diagnosis, sharper alignment to strategic priorities, explicit performance definitions and measurable behavioural outcomes.

An independent perspective brings structured objectivity, difficult questions and the distance required to identify structural misalignment. Olimpia works alongside your L&D team to audit and strengthen the learning system.

Independent performance assurance: a strategic audit of organisational capability, rather than a training or HR coaching programme.
