01 / A DIFFERENT QUESTION

When uncertainty slows decisions, L&D needs a clearer basis for its own.

What is helping the business perform—and what evidence do we have?

Under pressure, organisations can hesitate to invest, postpone programmes or keep familiar initiatives running. For L&D, the challenge is knowing what to protect, what to rethink and where to focus. Those decisions become harder when participation and positive feedback tell us little about what changes at work.

This brings us back to a fundamental question: does learning build the capabilities the business needs? Programmes may be well delivered, yet the connection between learning, everyday behaviour and business performance can remain unclear.

Our strategic audit examines that connection. We review how learning priorities are set, how programmes are designed and what evidence exists of application and sustained behavioural change. Working alongside your L&D team, Olimpia identifies strengths, gaps and practical adjustments.

The result is a clearer basis for action: what to retain, what to improve and what to investigate before committing further resources.

Independent performance assurance: a strategic audit of organisational capability, with evidence and a roadmap to guide your next decisions.
